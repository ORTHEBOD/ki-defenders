Ki Defenders – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Ki Defenders is a tower defense game for the web, Android and Windows. This page explains what information the game uses and why. We keep it to what the game needs.

Your player account

To save your progress online and show the leaderboard, the game stores on its server:

  • the player name you choose (shown on the leaderboard),
  • your PIN, stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form that cannot be read back,
  • your game progress: stars, coins, heroes, gear, kills and best Endless wave.

Your progress is also saved on your own device so you can play offline.

Sign in with Google

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google tells the game a unique account number so it can find your player again. The game does not store your email address, your real name, your photo or anything else from your Google account.

Play statistics

The game uses Google Analytics to count how many people play and how the game is played: which levels are started, won or lost, how far players get in Endless, and whether the game runs on the web, Android or PC. This uses a random number made on your device, not your name or account. Google Analytics also sees basic technical information such as your approximate country and device type. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Ads

The Android app may show ads, including optional videos you can watch for in-game coins. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID. You can reset or limit that ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses information from ads.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't ask for your email, phone number, location or contacts.
  • There are no in-app purchases with real money.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. A player name and PIN are all that is needed to play online, and players can also play without going online.

Deleting your data

To have your player and its progress removed from the server, contact us using the link below and tell us your player name.
