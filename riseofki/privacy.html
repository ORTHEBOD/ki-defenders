Rise of Ki: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Rise of Ki ("the game") is made by ORTHEBOD. This page explains what the game stores about you and why.

What the game stores

  • Player name and PIN. You choose a player name and a 4-digit PIN to log in. The PIN is never stored as it is: only a salted hash of it is kept.
  • Google account ID (optional). If you use "Sign in with Google", the game receives an ID token from Google and keeps only your Google account's ID number, to recognize you next time. It does not keep your email, your name from Google or your contacts.
  • Game progress. Your characters (name, look, level, skills, items, quests, the map you are in).
  • Purchases. If you buy diamonds, Google Play takes the payment. The game server gets only the purchase receipt (product, order number and purchase token), checks it with Google and gives the diamonds. The developer never sees your card or bank details.
  • Settings. Sound, music, controls and the last server address, stored only on your device.

Where it is stored

When you play offline, everything stays on your device. When you play online, your account and characters are stored on the game server run by the developer. Other players see only your character's name, look and level while you play together.

What the game does not do

  • No ads and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics, no location and no access to your contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • Your data is not sold or shared with anyone, except Google checking a Google sign-in or a purchase.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete a character in the game at any time (Change character, then Delete). To delete your whole account from the server, email the developer at the address below with your player name, and it will be removed within 30 days: your account, all your characters, your diamonds and your purchase records (a record that a purchase was paid may be kept for up to 1 year for refunds and fraud checks). Offline data is removed when you uninstall the game.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
