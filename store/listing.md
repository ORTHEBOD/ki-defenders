## Main store listing

**App name** (max 30): Ki Defenders

**Short description** (max 80):
Anime tower defense: build fighters, power them up, transform and defend!

**Full description** (max 4000):
Defend your base in an action-packed anime tower defense!

Place your fighters along the road and stop waves of monsters before they reach your base. Every fighter earns EXP from the damage it deals, levels up, and can be upgraded with Ki. Power them all the way up through 6 forms – from Base to Super Form, Divine Form and the mighty Ascended Form!

FEATURES
• 5 fighters with their own style: Brawler, Beam Sniper, Barrage, Control and Support
• 6 transformation forms, each with a new look and stronger powers
• Heroes you control on the battlefield, with special abilities like Kai Wave, Flash Step, Power Up and Spirit Orb
• A big hero skill tree with 5 branches, including hero forms
• Gear for your heroes: weapons, armor, pants, boots, amulets and accessories – and you see them on your hero
• Story levels across different worlds, with bosses that stomp, stun, shield and summon
• Endless Battle – how many waves can you survive? Climb the online leaderboard!
• Daily rewards and a Lucky Spin to win new heroes
• Save your progress online and play on any device (name + PIN, or Google sign-in)
• Works offline too

Send us tips and bug reports right from the game – we read every message!

## Data safety form
- Data collected:
  - **App activity / app interactions** (Google Analytics play stats) – used for analytics, not shared for ads
  - **Device or other IDs** (Analytics device id)
  - **Personal info: name** – the player name the player chooses (shown on the leaderboard)
  - **User IDs** only when Google sign-in is used (Google's account number is stored to find the player; the email is not saved)
  - **Messages** – Tips & bugs messages the player sends
- Data is sent over HTTPS (encrypted in transit).
- Players can ask to delete their data by email (orthebod2@gmail.com).

## Upload
- File: ki-defenders.aab from the newest release at
  https://github.com/ORTHEBOD/ki-defenders/releases/latest
- Personal developer accounts must first run a **closed test with at least 12 testers for 14 days**
  before the game can go to Production.
- After the first upload: copy the **App signing key SHA-1** (Play Console → Test and release →
  App integrity) and add it as one more Android OAuth client in Google Cloud, or Google sign-in
  won't work in the Play Store version.
